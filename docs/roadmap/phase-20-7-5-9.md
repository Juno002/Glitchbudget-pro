## 2. Final decision on `loan`

`loan` is **historical compatibility**, not a currently supported operational feature.

Historical loan rows:

- remain importable/exportable;
- remain identifiable as loans;
- are shown read-only;
- are not treated as credit cards;
- contribute any positive compatible balance to liabilities and net worth.

No UI for creating new loans is introduced.

## 3. Final debt semantics

Credit card:

- persisted `principal` means approved credit limit;
- signed balance = opening adjustment + linked credit purchases - linked payments;
- positive signed balance = liability;
- negative signed balance = cardholder surplus asset;
- credit limit itself never enters net worth.

Historical loan:

- persisted `principal` means original principal;
- compatible balance = `max(0, original principal + preserved opening adjustment - linked payments)`;
- no APR interest is synthesized;
- positive compatible balance remains a liability even if lifecycle status is closed.

Outside the compatibility boundary, callers use explicit names such as `cardCreditLimit` and `loanOriginalPrincipal`.

## 4. Final financial-date contract

New financial movements use:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD local civil date
```

No implicit UTC conversion determines the accounting day.

Legacy ISO datetimes are normalized once at the compatibility/import/migration boundary. Dexie v15 migrated legacy debt-payment datetimes to the canonical date-only contract.

## 11. Remaining known risks / deferred work

These are known constraints, not blockers for 20.7.5 closure:

- encrypted envelope v2 is deferred until representative physical-mobile KDF benchmarking justifies a concrete work factor;
- `loan` remains historical compatibility and is not a new-loan product feature;
- the performance baseline is CI/desktop-class evidence, not a low-end mobile hardware benchmark;
- the supported financial position remains single-currency; real FX/multi-currency is intentionally out of scope;
- local browser persistence still depends on browser/platform storage behavior, so external backups remain operationally important;
- the build currently relies on `next/font`; transient provider/loader failures have occurred in CI and are operational build noise rather than a financial-integrity defect.

## Architecture and privacy closure

The existing gates remain active:

- local-only source guard blocks network primitives in application code;
- generated static HTML requires `connect-src 'none'`;
- React surfaces have no direct financial Dexie access;
- canonical domain/services remain React-free;
- no new financial formula is introduced in UI;
- benchmark and golden reconciliation execute in the normal Quality checks path.
